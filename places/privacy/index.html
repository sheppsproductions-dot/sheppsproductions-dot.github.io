Privacy policy

Updated October 2, 2026

The short version

Places keeps your lists so you can use them on any phone. We don’t show ads, we don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

What we keep

Your email address, if you sign in, so you can get back into your account. Your lists: the places you save, their names and map positions, and the notes you write. When you mark a place “Been here”: the date, your stars and note, and any photos you add (made smaller before upload). The name you choose for shared lists. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we get your email address (Apple can give a private relay address instead) and your first name, which becomes your name on shared lists unless you choose another. If you use Places without signing in, your lists are kept under a guest account on our server until you sign in or delete it.

Your location

Location is optional. If you allow it, Places uses it while you’re using the app to find matches near you, to sort places by distance and to show where you are on the map. When you search, your location is sent with the search to help find nearby places. Places never saves your location.

Shared lists

When you invite someone to a list, they see that list: its places, the notes on them, who added each one, and the visits, stars, notes and photos people add there, with the name you chose. Only the people on that list see it. You can remove people, and anyone can leave.

Group votes

When you start a vote, anyone with its link sees the question, the list’s name and the names of the places you picked, and can vote without an account. They don’t see your notes or anything else on the list. Voters, in the app or on the web page, can give a first name if they want; everyone with the link sees it. A vote ends after two weeks, or when you end it.

Sharing into Places

When you share a link or some text into Places from another app (Safari, Maps, Messages and so on), the link goes to our server, which reads that web page’s public title, such as a restaurant’s name, to find the place. Shared text is only searched (with Google, like any search) when you tap Search. A screenshot you share is read on your iPhone with Apple’s text recognition, and if Apple Intelligence is on, it picks out place names on your iPhone too: the picture and its words never leave your phone, apart from a place name you choose to search. Places keeps the link and the words you shared with the place, as your note. Nothing else from the other app is read.

Who else handles your information

Supabase stores your account and lists on our behalf. Google provides place search, place details and photos: what you type in a search, and your location if you’ve allowed it, go to Google to answer it. Google’s own privacy policy applies to that. If you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, Apple or Google confirms who you are.

Deleting your information

Remove any place or list whenever you like. To delete your account and everything in it, open the You tab and choose Delete account (or, as a guest, Delete everything). It can’t be undone.

Children

Places isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and show the new date. Questions? Email sheppardrty@gmail.com.
